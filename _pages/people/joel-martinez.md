- **2025** — Machine Learning internship at Strategy& (PwC), developing forecasting models for rail transport demand
- **2024–2025** — M.Sc. in Mathematics and Applications (UAM, Madrid). Master's thesis on neural ODEs and generalization bounds
- **2023–2024** — Research internship at ICMM-CSIC, modeling bichromatically driven semiconductor spin qubits
- **2020–2024** — B.Sc. in Physics (UAM, Madrid), including an Erasmus year at Imperial College London. Bachelor's thesis on quantum dots as semiconductor spin qubits
