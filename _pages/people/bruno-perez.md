## Short CV

- **Present** — PhD student at IFIMAC, Universidad Autónoma de Madrid, co-supervised with Miguel Bello
- **2025–2026** — M.Sc. in Quantum Technologies (UIMP), covering quantum computation, algorithms, cryptography, thermodynamics, and information
- **2025–2026** — Quantum Technologies research fellow at IFF-CSIC, developing control techniques and quantum operations for hybrid quantum acoustic devices
- **Summer 2025** — Quantum Control research fellow at DIPC, simulating ESR-STM platforms and implementing quantum gates and algorithms in atomic and molecular spin qubits
- **2024–2025** — Research assistant in the Laser Spectroscopy Group at UAM, studying all-optical charge injection in two-dimensional semiconductors and developing automated data-processing tools
- **2020–2025** — B.Sc. in Physics (UAM, Madrid), including international exchanges at the University of Buenos Aires and the University of Palermo
- **2024–2025** — Nicolás Cabrera Award for Young Researchers and Comunidad de Madrid research scholarship for students of excellence

## Research interests

- Quantum control and quantum information
- Open quantum systems and quantum thermodynamics
- Hybrid quantum platforms, including quantum acoustic devices, superconducting circuits, and spin qubits
- Scientific computing, nanophotonics, and laser spectroscopy
